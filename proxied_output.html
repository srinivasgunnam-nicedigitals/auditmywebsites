Steps To Take After a Car Wreck in Texas

  • Make sure the scene is safe and that everyone is okay. Stay in your car if you don’t feel safe.
  • Exchange information - both insurance & personal contact information
  • If there are witnesses, be sure to get their contact information. Record the interaction if you can.
  • Once the police arrive to investigate the scene, get their name, badge number and direct phone number.
  • Be sure to keep your comments limited so they can’t be used against you later on.
  • Get medical attention immediately.
  • Contact Chad Jones Law to represent you!
